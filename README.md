# ORCA-MI Web

**Open Radiology & Computational Analysis — Molecular Imaging**, in the browser: a viewer (NIfTI, DICOM, PET/CT
fusion, 3D MIP, save as NIfTI) and the NEMA IEC phantom analysis (contrast recovery, background variability,
PDF / Excel reports).

**Open it:** https://orca-mi.github.io/orca-mi-web-limited/ (nothing to install; Chrome, Edge or Firefox)

- Your images never leave your computer: they are opened and processed inside your browser, nothing is uploaded.
- Open DICOM files, a whole folder, a zip, or NIfTI files (button or drag & drop).
- The full desktop application (AI, segmentation, registration, dosimetry …): https://github.com/orca-mi/orca-mi

**Research use only — not for clinical diagnosis.**
